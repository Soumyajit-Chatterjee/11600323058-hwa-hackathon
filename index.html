How it works

Three parts, one game that moves through your whole house.

1

Think

A chew-resistant base with puzzle chambers you can rearrange as your dog improves. Its ratchet release makes treats unpredictable, so every attempt feels different.

2

Feel

Hide five or six washable pods around the house. Each holds a piece of your clothing and a treat, turning your home into a scent-led search game.

3

Solve

Lay a path between pods with reusable scent wax sachets. It's an optional add-on for dogs who enjoy a longer route and more searching.

4

Recatch

Between hunts, your dog can come back to the den's warmth insert and mechanical pulse disc. A familiar resting point without electronics or any hazardous items.
